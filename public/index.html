How severity works

High: could block launch or directly harm people, with no safeguard described. Medium: plausibly applies; mitigate before scaling. Low: good practice or conditional. Severity is an AI judgment against this rubric, so check it. The human-review flag is rule-based: it triggers on low confidence or a missing source.
